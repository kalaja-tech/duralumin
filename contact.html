FAQs

Good questions.

Is the survey really free?

Yes. A designer visits, measures everything and talks you through the options. You'll get a written, fixed quote with no obligation and no pressure.

How long does it take from order to fitting?

Usually 6–10 weeks depending on the material and colour. Standard white uPVC can be quicker; bespoke bronze and steel take a little longer.

Will it be messy?

We put dust sheets down in every room we work in, take away all the old frames and leave everything clean. Most houses are finished in a single day.

Do I need planning permission?

Usually not for like-for-like replacements. In conservation areas or listed buildings you may need consent — we'll advise and can help with the application.

What does the guarantee cover?

Frames, glass units, hardware and installation for up to 20 years, insurance-backed so you're protected whatever happens. It transfers to the new owner if you move.

Do you offer finance?

Yes, we offer interest-free and low-rate finance options, subject to status. Ask your designer for details at the survey.
