Family-run, since 1998.

Three generations, one workshop, and a simple idea: the people who make your windows should be the people who fit them.

“We'd rather fit one door perfectly than ten in a hurry.”

David HargreavesFounder

Duralumin started in a single-bay workshop in Leeds, making timber sash windows for the terraces around Headingley. Word spread, and by 2004 we'd moved into our first proper factory.

Today we make aluminium, uPVC, timber and steel-look systems under one roof, and we still do everything ourselves — surveying, design, manufacture and installation. No subcontractors, no handing you over to someone else.

That's how we can offer a 20-year guarantee, and why most of our work comes from recommendations.

Our story

Aluminium line
